=== Task 3: Forward Traversal ===
Q: Which pointer moves forward? next
Forward: Song A Song B Song C Song D Song E 

=== Task 4: Backward Traversal ===

Backward: Song E Song D Song C Song B Song A 

=== Task 5: Insert Song X between B and C ===

=== Task 6: Delete Song C ===

Forward: Song A Song B Song X Song D Song E
